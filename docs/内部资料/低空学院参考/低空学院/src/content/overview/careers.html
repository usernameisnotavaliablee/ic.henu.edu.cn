招聘岗位与待遇Positions & Benefits

岗位：至善特聘教授、百人计划 A/B 岗、青年教师、博士后研究人员Positions: Zhishan Distinguished Professors, Hundred-Talent Plan A/B, Young Faculty, Postdoctoral Researchers

学校设立至善特聘教授，通过海内外引进、校内遴选等方式培养学科领军人才、学科带头人和优秀学术骨干，并通过“百人计划”引进和培养发展潜力较大的优秀青年学者。以下待遇适用于通过校学术委员会评审、符合学校发展和建设需要的受聘人选。The university appoints Zhishan Distinguished Professors to attract and cultivate discipline leaders and outstanding academic backbones, and recruits promising young scholars through the Hundred-Talent Plan. The following benefits apply to candidates approved by the university academic committee and aligned with university development needs.

（一）至善特聘教授(1) Zhishan Distinguished Professors

卓越人才岗Excellence Tier

条件Requirements

在本学科领域具有国内外公认的学术声望和学术影响力，为所在专业领域和学科的一流学者，取得重大标志性研究成果。Recognized academic reputation and influence at home and abroad, with landmark research achievements and first-class standing in the relevant discipline.

薪酬待遇Compensation

享受有竞争力的年薪；提供住房（或住房补贴）及丰厚的安家费、充足的科研启动经费及平台建设经费；根据实际情况解决或协助解决配偶工作；解决子女（中小学、幼儿园）入学问题；配备专业学术团队等。Competitive annual salary; housing or housing subsidy, generous relocation allowance, sufficient research start-up funding and platform support; assistance with spouse employment when applicable; schooling support for children; and a dedicated academic team.

领军人才岗Leading Talent Tier

条件Requirements

在本学科领域具有良好的学术声望和学术影响力，为所在专业领域的著名学者和学科带头人，取得突出的教学科研成果；原则上年龄不超过 60 周岁。Strong academic reputation and influence, recognized as a leading scholar or discipline leader with outstanding teaching and research achievements; normally under 60 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

享受有竞争力的年薪；提供住房（或住房补贴）及丰厚的安家费、充足的科研启动经费及平台建设经费；根据实际情况解决或协助解决配偶工作；解决子女（中小学、幼儿园）入学问题；配备专业学术团队等。Competitive annual salary; housing or housing subsidy, generous relocation allowance, sufficient research start-up funding and platform support; assistance with spouse employment when applicable; schooling support for children; and a dedicated academic team.

拔尖人才岗Outstanding Talent Tier

条件Requirements

在本学科领域具有良好的学术影响力，为所在专业领域的知名学者或有潜力的中青年学者，取得较为突出的教学科研成果；原则上年龄不超过 50 周岁。Good academic influence, recognized scholar or promising young and mid-career scholar with notable teaching and research achievements; normally under 50 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

年薪不低于 70 万；提供丰厚的安家费及住房（或住房补贴）、充足的科研启动经费及平台建设经费；根据实际情况解决或协助解决配偶工作；解决子女（中小学、幼儿园）入学问题；配备专业学术团队等。Annual salary no less than RMB 700,000; generous relocation allowance and housing support, sufficient research start-up funding and platform support; assistance with spouse employment when applicable; schooling support for children; and a dedicated academic team.

（二）百人计划(2) Hundred-Talent Plan

百人计划 A 岗Hundred-Talent Plan A

条件Requirements

取得博士学位后，具有在海内外知名科研机构、高校或企业研发机构不少于 2 年的科研工作经历，并取得本学科领域高水平科研成果；原则上年龄不超过 38 周岁。After obtaining a PhD, at least two years of research experience at a well-known research institute, university, or corporate R&D organization, with high-level achievements in the field; normally under 38 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

年薪不低于 50 万；提供安家费及购房补贴、充足的科研启动经费；配偶学历为硕士研究生及以上者，解决工作；解决子女入学问题；配备专业学术团队；支持争取国家和地方人才计划项目。Annual salary no less than RMB 500,000; relocation and housing purchase subsidies, sufficient research start-up funding; employment support for spouses with a master degree or above; schooling support for children; dedicated academic team; and support for applying to national and local talent programs.

百人计划 B 岗Hundred-Talent Plan B

条件Requirements

具有在海内外知名大学、科研机构学习或工作 3 年以上经历，取得较为突出的研究成果；原则上年龄不超过 35 周岁。More than three years of study or work experience at a well-known university or research institute at home or abroad, with notable research achievements; normally under 35 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

年薪不低于 30 万；提供安家费及购房补贴；提供充足的科研启动经费；解决子女入学问题；支持争取国家和地方人才计划项目。Annual salary no less than RMB 300,000; relocation and housing purchase subsidies; sufficient research start-up funding; schooling support for children; and support for applying to national and local talent programs.

青年教师Young Faculty

条件Requirements

具有良好教育背景和学术发展潜力并取得代表性研究成果的优秀博士或高级职称人员，从事教学和科研工作，年龄不超过 35 岁。Outstanding PhD graduates or senior professionals with strong educational background, academic potential, and representative research achievements, engaged in teaching and research; under 35 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

年薪 17–24 万；提供安家费及购房补贴；提供科研启动经费；解决子女入学问题；支持争取国家和地方人才计划项目。Annual salary of RMB 170,000 to 240,000; relocation and housing purchase subsidies; research start-up funding; schooling support for children; and support for applying to national and local talent programs.

（三）博士后研究人员 / 专职科研人员(3) Postdoctoral / Full-time Research Staff

博士后研究人员Postdoctoral Researcher

条件Requirements

具备从事教学科研工作的能力和素质；博士学位获得时间原则上不超过 3 年，年龄不超过 35 周岁。Capability and qualifications for teaching and research; PhD obtained within the last three years in principle; under 35 years old.

薪酬待遇Compensation

实行年薪制，提供优良的科研和工作环境；享受工作任务外的绩效；符合省市人才政策的，可享受相应人才待遇；享受子女在附属学校（幼儿园、附属小学）入学方面的待遇；优秀者可留校工作。Annual salary system with excellent research and working conditions; performance rewards beyond assigned duties; eligible candidates may enjoy provincial or municipal talent benefits; schooling support at affiliated schools; outstanding fellows may be retained by the university.

专职科研人员Full-time Research Staff

条件Requirements

具有良好教育背景和学术发展潜力并取得有一定代表性的研究成果，专职从事科研工作，年龄不超过 35 周岁。Strong educational background, academic potential, and representative research achievements; engaged full-time in research; under 35 years old.

招聘程序Recruitment Process

报名 → 考核 → 体检和考察 → 公示Apply → Assessment → Health Check & Review → Public Notice

报名Apply

应聘者将简历、相关证明材料等发送至相应邮箱。简历内容应包括：个人基本信息、学习和工作经历、科研成果情况等。Applicants should send their CV and supporting materials to the designated email address. The CV should include basic personal information, education and work experience, and research achievements.

考核Assessment

考核一般包括初选、面试等。主要考察应聘人员的思想品德素质、团队协作、专业技术水平等综合素质。Assessment generally includes preliminary review and interview, focusing on professional competence, teamwork, ethics, and overall qualifications.

体检和考察Health Check & Review

体检标准参照《河南省教师资格申请人员体格检查标准（2017 年修订）》执行。体检合格人员由我校对其思想政治表现、道德品质等方面进行考察。The health check follows the Physical Examination Standards for Teacher Qualification Applicants in Henan Province (2017 revision). Candidates who pass the health check will undergo a university review of political conduct, ethics, and related qualifications.

公示Public Notice

体检和考察均合格人员经学校研究后确定为拟聘用人员，在学校人才人事部网站进行公示，公示期为 7 个工作日。Candidates who pass both health check and review will be confirmed as proposed hires after university approval and announced on the university HR website for seven working days.
