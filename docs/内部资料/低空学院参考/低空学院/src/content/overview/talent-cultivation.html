学科专业Disciplines & Programs

本—硕—博贯通培养Bachelor–Master–Doctoral Pathway

学院围绕低空技术与空天信息领域，构建从本科到博士的完整人才培养链条。The college builds a complete talent cultivation chain from undergraduate to doctoral level, centered on low-altitude technology and aerospace information.

  • 低空技术与工程（本科）Low-Altitude Technology & Engineering (Bachelor's)
  • 低空技术与空天工程（硕士）Low-Altitude Technology & Aerospace Engineering (Master's)
  • 航空宇航科学与技术（博士）Aerospace Science & Technology (Doctoral)

科创实践Innovation Practice

面向低空技术与空天信息前沿，依托科研平台、工程项目和学科竞赛，组织学生参与真实课题、系统研制与数据处理全过程，在实践中提升问题发现、方案设计和工程实现能力。Focused on frontier areas of low-altitude technology and aerospace information, students engage in real research tasks, system development, and data processing through research platforms, engineering projects, and discipline competitions, strengthening their ability to identify problems, design solutions, and deliver engineering results.

  • 参与无人机系统、卫星载荷与遥感数据处理等科研训练Research training in UAV systems, satellite payloads, and remote sensing data processing
  • 围绕工程项目开展团队协作、方案论证与样机验证Team collaboration, solution review, and prototype validation through engineering projects
  • 支持学生参加创新创业训练计划、学科竞赛与开放课题Support for innovation and entrepreneurship training programs, discipline competitions, and open research topics

国际交流International Exchange

依托学校国际交流平台，支持学生参加校际互换、海外学习与联合培养项目，拓展全球视野与跨文化协作能力。Leveraging the university's international exchange platforms, supporting students in inter-university exchanges, overseas study, and joint training programs to broaden global perspectives and cross-cultural collaboration skills.

  • 国（境）外校际互换与交流学习Inter-university exchange & study abroad
