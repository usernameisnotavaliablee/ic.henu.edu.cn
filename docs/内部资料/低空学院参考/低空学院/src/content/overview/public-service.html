L1A 原始偏振数据Raw Polarization Data 完成数据解包、时间排序和 HDF5 标准封装，保留 0°、45°、90°、135°偏振方向原始观测值。Data unpacking, time sorting, and HDF5 standard packaging, preserving raw observations at 0°, 45°, 90°, 135° polarization angles. 保留可追溯观测底座，支撑科研复算、算法迭代和质量追踪。Preserves traceable observation baseline for research reprocessing, algorithm iteration, and quality tracking.
L1B 辐射度 / Stokes 产品Radiance / Stokes Products 完成辐射定标，输出 Radiance 单位的 Stokes 矢量 I/Q/U 分量。Radiometric calibration, outputting Stokes vector I/Q/U components in Radiance units. 为定量遥感、跨时相对比和参数反演提供物理量输入。Provides physical inputs for quantitative RS, cross-temporal comparison, and parameter retrieval.
L1C 几何定位产品Geometric Positioning Products 结合轨道姿态、成像几何和辅助掩膜，输出角度、海陆掩码、质量标识等定位信息。Combining orbit/attitude, imaging geometry, and auxiliary masks to output angles, land/sea masks, and quality flags. 支持数据浏览、空间检索和后续几何精校正。Supports data browsing, spatial search, and subsequent geometric refinement.
L1D 几何精校正产品Geometric Refinement Products 引入地面控制点 GCP 修正几何误差，生成高精度几何数据。GCP-based geometric error correction, producing high-precision geometric data. 提升位置精度，满足制图、监测和变化分析需求。Improves positional accuracy for mapping, monitoring, and change analysis.
L1E 投影校正产品 / 地面网格Projection-Corrected / Gridded Products 完成从像元坐标到标准地理 / 地图投影网格的转换。Pixel-to-standard geographic/map projection grid transformation. 让产品可直接进入 GIS、Web 地图和公共数据服务。Enables direct ingestion into GIS, Web maps, and public data services.
L1F 双相机融合数据产品 / 地面网格Dual-Camera Fused / Gridded Products 实现载荷内双相机观测数据的协同配准与融合。Co-registration and fusion of dual-camera observations within the payload. 形成多角度、多波段一致化数据，为 L2 反演提供更完整约束。Produces multi-angle, multi-band consistent data, providing more complete constraints for L2 retrieval.
