创新单元Innovation Units

低空技术创新Low-Altitude Tech Innovation

面向低空经济与通用航空发展需求，聚焦飞行平台、运行保障和基础设施三大方向，开展关键核心技术攻关，支撑应急救援、生态监测、城市治理、资源巡检等典型应用场景。Serving the development needs of the low-altitude economy and general aviation, this unit focuses on flight platforms, operational support, and infrastructure, tackling key core technologies for typical application scenarios such as emergency rescue, ecological monitoring, urban governance, and resource inspection.

  • 飞行平台：长航时新能源无人机、智能飞行平台与任务载荷Flight platforms: long-endurance new-energy UAVs, intelligent flight platforms, and mission payloads
  • 运行保障：低空空域协同、低空气象保障Operational support: low-altitude airspace coordination and low-altitude meteorological support
  • 基础设施：低空立体交通基础设施Infrastructure: low-altitude three-dimensional transport infrastructure

空天信息创新Aerospace Info Innovation

面向国家空间基础设施建设和空天信息产业发展需求，聚焦卫星任务、地面数据处理系统、太空算力和行业应用等方向，开展关键核心技术攻关，形成覆盖卫星研制、在轨测试、数据处理和行业应用的全链条创新能力。Serving national space infrastructure construction and the development needs of the aerospace information industry, this unit focuses on satellite missions, information processing, and industry applications, tackling key core technologies and building full-chain innovation capabilities covering satellite development, on-orbit testing, data processing, and industry applications.

  • 卫星任务：卫星总体设计、遥感载荷、在轨测试与地面支撑Satellite missions: overall satellite design, remote sensing payloads, on-orbit testing, and ground support
  • 地面数据处理系统：数据处理系统、智能反演与产品生成、通信卫星Information processing: data processing systems, intelligent retrieval, and product generation
  • 太空算力：星上智能计算、在轨协同计算Space computing: onboard intelligent computing and in-orbit collaborative computing
  • 真实性检验：在轨定标和检验、真实性检验场站网、标准数据和基准Validation: on-orbit calibration and verification, validation site network, standard data and benchmarks
  • 行业应用：生态环境、双碳监测、资源调查、防灾减灾等产品Industry applications: ecological environment, carbon peaking and carbon neutrality monitoring, resource surveys, disaster prevention and mitigation products, and more
